## 📌 Overview

**ScamShield AI** is an AI-powered fake news, scam message, and malicious URL detection platform with a WhatsApp chatbot interface.

Online fraud, phishing attacks, fake job offers, fraudulent investment schemes, misleading news, and suspicious forwarded messages are common digital safety challenges. ScamShield AI aims to help users identify potential threats by analyzing suspicious messages and providing understandable risk assessments.

Users can submit suspicious text messages, news claims, and URLs through a web dashboard or a configured WhatsApp chatbot. The system analyzes the content and returns an estimated risk score, detected warning signs, classification, and recommended safety actions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Detect potentially fraudulent messages and phishing attempts.
- Identify suspicious URLs and deceptive domain patterns.
- Analyze news-like claims for potential misinformation.
- Provide explainable risk assessments using AI, NLP, and rule-based analysis.
- Integrate a WhatsApp chatbot using the Twilio API.
- Maintain analysis history through a database.
- Promote safer online communication and digital awareness.
